Our Story

Saying 'oui' à Paris.

After 16 years together (nearly half our lives) and 10 years of PACS, we’ve shared more laughter, adventures, and beautiful moments than we could ever count. As it happens, 2026 is also the year both of us became French 🇫🇷, which felt like the perfect excuse for a celebration in Paris. This won’t be a traditional wedding - just a celebration that feels a little more like a birthday party. No stress. No pressure. No dress code. Just come as you are.
