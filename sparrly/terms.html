2. Description of service

Sparrly is an AI-powered conversational sparring simulator designed for engineering and product managers to build behavioral muscle memory for difficult workplace interactions. Key features include:

  • Simulated workplace conversations (delivering critical performance feedback, saying no to scope creep, boundary setting, cross-functional alignment)
  • Interactive AI counterparty personas with adaptive resistance and realistic conversational dynamics
  • Testing and practicing leadership communication frameworks (e.g. Radical Candor, Situation-Behavior-Impact)
  • Speech-to-text voice input transcription and natural audio playback
  • Session history review, replay, and repetition practice
